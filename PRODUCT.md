# Product Context

## Product register

Product surface. This is a compact browser utility used inside Pinterest, not a marketing page.

## Users

- People collecting visual references from Pinterest in Chrome or Edge.
- They may be logged in or logged out and may save one item or an entire board/feed.
- They value original quality, minimal interaction, clear progress, and dependable recovery more than advanced configuration.

## Jobs to be done

- Collect the currently visible Pinterest media with as little manual work as possible.
- Download original images, GIFs, and available videos without recompression.
- Package batch results into one readable zip file.
- Avoid re-downloading saved or previously downloaded items when desired.
- Make failures understandable without exposing technical detail during normal use.

## Brand personality

- Calm, capable, restrained, and crafted.
- AndDream branding should feel like a quiet maker's signature, never an advertisement.
- Indigo is the default identity; alternate themes are optional expressions, not a multicolor visual system.

## Anti-references

- Pinterest-red-dominated controls that make the tool look native to Pinterest.
- Rainbow gradients or several competing accent colors in one theme.
- Technical logs occupying the main workflow.
- Decorative glass effects, large headings, excessive pills, and ornamental animation.
- Disabled primary actions with no explanation.

## Design principles

1. One obvious next action at every state.
2. Reveal technical detail only when requested.
3. Status and recovery must remain visible during long operations.
4. Keep the panel compact, but never compress controls below comfortable click targets.
5. Brand through typography, color, and restraint rather than decoration.

## Accessibility

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast.
- Preserve keyboard focus visibility and semantic button controls.
- Do not communicate state by color alone.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences; animations are optional and subtle.
- Keep interactive targets at least 36px high in the compact desktop panel.
